Research in detail →

We study how transcription factors and cofactors regulate gene expression and shape cell fate, and we design new regulators to explore how cell states change in aging and neurodegenerative disease.

  • Understand: Starting from protein sequences and molecular interactions, we trace how gene regulation connects to cell fate.
  • Design: We design and synthesize regulators, and use high-throughput experiments to learn how to change cell states on purpose.
  • Integrate: We connect evidence across molecules, cells and organisms, build models, and propose and test new hypotheses.
